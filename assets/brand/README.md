# Golem brand

The official Golem logo is a hand-lettered wordmark: **go** in Go cyan,
**lem** in ink, and an **e** that is a little golem itself — a stone bowl for
a head, the crossbar a mouth, and two cyan eyes set into its brow. A golem is
a creature of clay brought to life by an inscribed word; the framework's
promise is the same — compose typed parts, run, get a dependable result. The
name carries **go** in its first two letters, and the wordmark carries the
golem's face in its fourth.

All letterforms are drawn as paths. There is no brand typeface to install or
license; the SVGs are the source of truth.

## Official assets

| Asset | File | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark (light backgrounds) | `logo/golem-wordmark.svg` | Primary logo: README, docs, decks |
| Wordmark (dark backgrounds) | `logo/golem-wordmark-dark.svg` | Dark-mode README, dark surfaces |
| Wordmark PNG exports | `logo/golem-wordmark.png`, `logo/golem-wordmark-dark.png` (1200 px wide, transparent) | Places SVG is unsupported |
| Mark (light) | `logo/golem-mark.svg` / `logo/golem-mark.png` (512 px) | Square contexts on light backgrounds |
| Mark (dark badge) | `logo/golem-mark-dark.svg` / `logo/golem-mark-dark.png` (512 px) | GitHub/org avatar, app icon, favicon source |
| Social card | `social/golem-social-card.png` (1280×640) | GitHub social preview, Open Graph, link unfurls |

The mark is the wordmark's `e` alone: the golem face at avatar scale. The docs
site favicon and header logo (`docs/guides/assets/images/`) are generated from
it.

## Usage rules

- **Clear space:** keep at least the x-height of the wordmark (13% of its
  width) free on all sides; for the mark, 12% of its width.
- **Minimum sizes:** wordmark at least 110 px wide on screen; the mark stays
  legible to 16 px.
- **Variants:** use the light wordmark on light backgrounds and the dark
  wordmark on Night or similarly dark backgrounds. In square or circular
  contexts, use the mark, never a squeezed wordmark.
- **Do not:** recolor, rotate, skew, stretch, outline, add shadows or glows,
  set the name in another typeface next to the logo, or place it on a busy
  background without a contrasting variant.

## Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex | RGB | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Go Cyan | `#00ADD8` | 0, 173, 216 | The `go` letters, the e's eyes, accents |
| Primary support | Spark | `#5DC9E2` | 93, 201, 226 | Highlights on cyan |
| Neutral | Ink | `#232B38` | 35, 43, 56 | The `lem` letters, text on light |
| Neutral | Stone | `#5B6678` | 91, 102, 120 | The mark's glyph on light |
| Neutral | Clay Shadow | `#4B5566` | 75, 85, 102 | Deep neutral accents |
| Surface | Night | `#141A26` | 20, 26, 38 | Dark badge background, dark surfaces |
| On-dark text | Mist | `#E8EDF4` | 232, 237, 244 | The `lem` letters on dark |

Go Cyan is the Go ecosystem's brand color; using it signals Go-native without
imitating any Go-adjacent product mark.
